AI Tools: A Tool, Not a Crutch

Artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot can help students understand difficult topics and improve their learning. However, students should not depend on these tools to do all their work.

As a Computer Science student, I can use AI to explain programming concepts, identify errors in my code and provide practice questions. I should still write code, test it and make sure I understand how it works.

AI can also make mistakes, so I should check its answers against textbooks, lectures and reliable sources. I must follow my university's rules about academic honesty and acknowledge AI assistance when required.

In my opinion, AI is most useful when it helps me learn how to solve a problem instead of simply giving me an answer. This will help me become a more independent student and a better programmer.
